# Employee Benefits FAQ

**Company:** Meridian Software Services Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** HR-FAQ-001  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective date:** 2026-04-01  
**Owner:** Human Resources  
**Applies to:** All permanent employees  

### What health insurance does Meridian provide?

Every permanent employee is covered under a group health policy with a family floater of Rs 5,00,000, covering the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. The company pays the premium. Parents can be added at the employee's own cost during the annual enrolment window.

### Is there accident or life cover?

Yes. Group personal accident cover is Rs 20,00,000 and group term life cover is 3 times annual fixed pay. Both are paid for by the company.

### How does the provident fund work?

Both the employee and Meridian contribute 12 percent of basic pay to the Employees' Provident Fund each month. Gratuity is payable after 5 years of continuous service.

### What is the wellness allowance?

Each employee can claim up to Rs 10,000 per financial year for gym membership, fitness classes or health check-ups, with receipts submitted through the expense portal.

### What is the learning budget?

Each employee has a learning budget of Rs 30,000 per financial year for courses, certifications and books. Your manager approves the request before you pay.

### Is there a referral bonus?

Employees who refer a candidate who joins and completes 6 months receive Rs 25,000. For roles at Level 4 and above the bonus is Rs 40,000.

### When does insurance cover begin for a new joiner?

Cover starts on the date of joining. The e-cards are shared by HR within 10 working days.
