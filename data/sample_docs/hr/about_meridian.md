## Who we are

Meridian Software Services Pvt. Ltd. is a software engineering company founded in 2012 and headquartered in Pune, with a second office in Bengaluru. We have about 400 employees and build software for customers in banking, logistics and healthcare.

## What we do

- Custom software engineering and product development.
- Cloud migration and platform engineering.
- Data platforms and analytics.

## How we are organised

The company has five departments: Human Resources, Finance, Information Technology, Engineering and Sales. Each department is led by a Department Head. Teams within a department are led by managers, and individual contributors are members.

## Our values

- Own it: take responsibility for outcomes.
- Be curious: ask questions and keep learning.
- Build trust: be honest with customers and colleagues.
- Ship with care: quality matters as much as speed.
