ANNOUNCEMENT: ENGINEERING REORGANISATION FROM 1 AUGUST 2026
Meridian Software Services Pvt. Ltd.

Document ID: HR-ANN-014
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-07-15
Owner: Office of the CEO
Applies to: All employees

Date: 15 July 2026. From: Office of the CEO.

WHAT IS CHANGING

From 1 August 2026 the Engineering department will be organised into two units: Platform Engineering, which owns cloud infrastructure, internal tooling and reliability, and Product Engineering, which owns customer-facing products.

WHAT IS NOT CHANGING

  - There are no role eliminations or salary changes as part of this reorganisation.
  - Existing leave balances, benefits and the on-call rotation continue as they are.
  - Departments other than Engineering are not affected.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

  1. Each employee in Engineering will be told their new unit and reporting manager by 24 July 2026.
  2. HR drop-in sessions will be held from 20 to 22 July in the Pune and Bengaluru offices.
  3. Questions can be sent to hr@meridian.example.
