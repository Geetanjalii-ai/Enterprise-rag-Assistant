# Password and Multi-Factor Authentication Guide

**Company:** Meridian Software Services Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** IT-HOW-001  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** 2026-03-01  
**Owner:** Information Technology  
**Applies to:** All employees  

## Password rules

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
- Do not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 180 days.
- Your account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
- Do not use your name, date of birth or the word Meridian in a password.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA is required for every employee on every Meridian account. SMS codes are not allowed. Use the Meridian Authenticator app on your phone.

## How to enrol in MFA

1. Sign in to the portal at id.meridian.example with your temporary password.
2. Open Security and choose Set up authenticator app.
3. Scan the QR code with the Meridian Authenticator app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm. Save your recovery codes in the company password vault.

## How to reset a forgotten password

1. Go to id.meridian.example and choose Forgot password.
2. Verify your identity with MFA.
3. Set a new password that follows the rules above.
4. If you cannot use MFA, call the IT Helpdesk on extension 5000.

## Password manager

Meridian provides a company password vault. Store all work passwords in it and never in plain text files or notes.
