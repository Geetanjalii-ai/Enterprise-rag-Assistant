# VPN Setup Guide

**Company:** Meridian Software Services Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** IT-HOW-002  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01  
**Owner:** Information Technology  
**Applies to:** All employees  

## When to use the VPN

Connect to the company VPN whenever you access internal systems from outside the office, including from home and while travelling.

## Install the VPN client

1. Open Software Centre on your company laptop.
2. Search for Meridian VPN and click Install.
3. Restart the laptop if asked.

## Connect

1. Open the Meridian VPN app.
2. Enter the server address vpn.meridian.example.
3. Sign in with your Meridian email and approve the MFA prompt.
4. Wait for the status to change to Connected.

## Troubleshooting

- Connection fails: check your internet and try another network.
- MFA prompt does not arrive: open the authenticator app and enter the code manually.
- VPN is slow: disconnect and reconnect, which selects the nearest gateway.
- Still stuck: raise a ticket with the IT Helpdesk, or call extension 5000.

## Good to know

The VPN is only supported on company laptops running Windows 11 or macOS 14 and later. Personal devices cannot connect.
