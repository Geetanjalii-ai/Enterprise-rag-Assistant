# IT Helpdesk FAQ

**Company:** Meridian Software Services Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** IT-FAQ-001  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective date:** 2026-05-01  
**Owner:** Information Technology  
**Applies to:** All employees  

### What are the helpdesk hours and how do I contact it?

The IT Helpdesk is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00 IST. Call extension 5000, email helpdesk@meridian.example or raise a ticket in the IT portal.

### How quickly will my ticket be answered?

| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Company-wide outage, security incident | Within 1 hour |
| P2 High | You cannot work at all | Within 4 hours |
| P3 Normal | Question or minor problem | Within 1 business day |

### How do I request new software?

Raise a ticket with your manager's approval. Requests for software outside the catalogue are reviewed in 3 working days.

### What is the Wi-Fi at the office?

Use Meridian-Corp with your company login. Visitors use Meridian-Guest, which gives internet access only and expires after 24 hours.

### My laptop is lost or stolen. What do I do?

Call the IT Helpdesk immediately and within 1 hour at the latest. Then follow the Laptop Request and Replacement Process.

### I got a suspicious email. What should I do?

Do not click anything. Use the Report Phish button or forward it to security@meridian.example.
